# Arithmetic review and revision: ECC2K-130 on Blackwell GPUs

Reviewed 2026-10-01. This review follows `REVIEW-20260926.md` and resolves the
blocking issue it raised. It checks the GPU arithmetic claims independently,
re-reads the walk claims against current results in `aburan28/crypto`
(`ecc2k130/WALK-CONSTANT.md`, `ecc2k130/CYCLE-ESCAPE-V3.md`), and records
what changed in `paper.tex`.

**Verdict:**

- The arithmetic is correct.
- The headline was not ready as written. The walk it measured lets
  fruitless cycles through, and the manuscript underestimated them by about
  an order of magnitude.
- The revision takes route (b) of the 2026-09-26 review. It leads with the
  campaign's Frobenius walk at 17.63 B/s, where the collision behaviour is
  known, and reports the table walk's 22.10 B/s as an arithmetic ceiling.
- No new GPU measurement was needed or made.

- the modulus confirmed irreducible.

It needed a one-line fix first, because its archive path pointed at
`ecc2k130/research/...` instead of `ecc2k130/runner/research/...`.

The trace argument for selector totality is also correct. On
`y^2 + xy = x^3 + a_2 x^2 + a_6`, a point is a double iff `Tr(x) = Tr(a_2)`.
Every element of a normal basis has the same trace, which is nonzero. So
`Tr(x) ≡ w (mod 2)` and `w` is even on the subgroup.

## 2. Blocking: the fruitless-cycle estimate

The manuscript's §3.3 estimated two figures:

- about 1% of DP34 trails trapped in six-step cycles;
- about 0.1% of merges failing to coalesce.

It modelled only pairwise cancellations. It missed the Frobenius relation.
On this curve `σ² + σ + 2 = 0` as an endomorphism, hence also
`σ³ + σ − 2 = 0`. As a result, four steps in one branch with addends
`σ^{k+2}T, σ^{k+1}T, σ^kT, σ^kT` return a walk to its start. So do
`σ^{k+3}T, σ^{k+1}T, −σ^kT, −σ^kT`. There are 24 such ordered patterns, and
no two of their tags cancel, so the v1 rule never fires on them.

The companion repository counted those patterns and measured them on the
device walk and on an emulation. It saw 1,145 τ-relation returns against
1,312 predicted. Its trap-cost model at `n = 131`, `H = 8`, with
ECC2K-130's branch distribution, gives:

| `dpWeight` | Walk | Trails trapped | Loss at best guard |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 34 (this paper's benchmark threshold) | Table walk with rule v1 | **11.6%** | ×1.655 |
| 32 (live campaign) | Table walk with rule v1 | **53.7%** | ×5.33 |
| 32 | Frobenius walk | 0 | ×1.000 at `maxIters = 2^32` |

Priced end to end at DP32, the table walk as measured costs **4.85–6.26×
the Frobenius walk per solve** (`WALK-CONSTANT.md` §6). The manuscript's 1%
was therefore about 10× low at DP34, and it missed the dominant family.

Since 2026-09-26 the rule has gone through v2 and then v3:

- v2 added the τ-relations and a four-tag window.
- v3 adds a raw-cycle anchor so the exit does not depend on the entry
  (`CYCLE-ESCAPE-V3.md`).
- v3 is projected at 0.81–0.86× the Frobenius walk's cost per solve.
- Its GPU throughput is **unmeasured**, and v2 throughput does not transfer
  to it.

The 22.10 B/s figure therefore cannot carry a collision-search claim until
v3 runs on the card.

## 3. Attribution of the 20× factor (also affects the blog draft)

"Native carry-less multiplication beats 2009-style bitslicing by about 20×"
over-attributes the gain. The packed-backend ledger
(`paper/sec-ecc2k-gpu.tex`, Table "Packed-backend rungs";
`crypto:ecc2k130/THROUGHPUT-30B.md`) shows:

- The bitsliced control measured 0.852294 B/s. It used CUDA 12.8.1 and an
  older source, and it is not paired with any later row.
- Packing plus software carry-less products and layout reached 7.110440 B/s.
- **`clmad` itself, at the same layout, took 7.11 to 8.70 B/s (+22.4%)**
  while cutting instructions by 47%.
- Batch 16, compact state and shared Frobenius masks then took the rate to
  14.64 B/s (+68%), with essentially unchanged instruction count.

So the factor of about 21 from the bitsliced control to 17.63 B/s belongs to
the whole packed design. `clmad` is one part of it, and it created headroom
that the layout work then consumed. The revised paper says this. The blog
draft (`docs/blog/2026-ecc2k130-cost-DRAFT.md`) is corrected in the same
change.

## 4. What the revision changes in `paper.tex`

1. **Headline.** The paper now leads with the Frobenius walk
   `R ← σ^j(R) + R`, `j = 3 + ((HW(x)/2) mod 8)`. This is Bailey et al.'s
   iteration function and the walk the live campaign runs:
   - 17.625650 B/s median of five paired runs;
   - every pair +2.72% to +2.85% over the deployed v1 build;
   - DP32 collection active, 705 records per run, zero drops;
   - receipt `production/2026-09-21-frobenius-fused-confirm.json`.

   Its iteration constant is measured, `c = 1.08–1.10` at `n = 23–59`, and
   it has no fruitless cycles.
2. **The table walk** keeps its full measurement (22.100934 B/s, all five
   samples, the screening ladder). It is now framed as the arithmetic
   ceiling of a walk whose collision efficiency is not yet established.
3. **§3.3 is rewritten** with the τ-relation family and the trap and
   coalescence figures from the companion measurements. The heuristic 1%
   and 0.1% figures are removed.
4. **Cost to solve.** `2^60.9` iterations at 17.63 B/s is 3.87 GPU-years on
   one card. That rests on the Frobenius walk only, with `maxIters = 2^32`
   so the guard costs 0.01% of steps.
5. **Packed-backend ledger.** A table of the adjacent paired steps is added,
   with the attribution caveat from §3.
6. **Related work.** The missing items from the 2026-09-26 review are added:
   - Bos–Kleinjung–Lenstra on the negation map;
   - Gallant–Lambert–Vanstone and Wiener–Zuccherato on Frobenius classes;
   - Fan et al. and Bernstein et al. on FPGAs;
   - Wenger–Wolfger;
   - the 2012/137 assembly paper;
   - NVIDIA's `clmad` announcement.
7. **Housekeeping.**
   - The `bernstein2012` entry now carries its venue year (INDOCRYPT 2010).
   - The artifact paths now read `ecc2k130/runner/research/...`.
   - The date and status are updated.

## 5. What is still needed before submission

- **A public deposit** of the source and receipts, such as Zenodo. The
  manuscript cites a local archive.
- **A read of the typeset build.** `paper.pdf` was rebuilt from revision 2
  with Tectonic 0.15.0. It is 22 pages, with no undefined references or
  citations and no overfull boxes. A human should still read it once.
- **A final bibliography check against publisher records.** The new
  entries carry identifiers (DOIs, ePrint numbers) that were checked on
  2026-09-26. Page ranges that were not checked have been left out rather
  than guessed.
- **Optional, strengthens the paper: a table-walk v3 run on the card.** This
  means the five-sample paired rate plus the collision-quality numbers on the
  scaled `m = 41` and `m = 83` curves. If v3 holds near its projection, the
  paper can upgrade the table walk from "ceiling" to "faster per solve".
  Without that run, the paper is complete as revised.
- **An author decision on clock telemetry.** The Frobenius-walk receipt has
  no clock telemetry, so its cycles-per-iteration figure is stated at the
  clock the table-walk session recorded and flagged as such.
