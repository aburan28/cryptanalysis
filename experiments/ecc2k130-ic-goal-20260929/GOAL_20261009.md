# ECC2K-130 IC goal: a target-coupled ordinary relation, then a complete one-target solve

The immediate algorithmic goal is an **unpinned, independently verified
12-summand factor-base relation for one frozen public point on the exact
degree-131 challenge curve**, found within 600 seconds of target-dependent
work and 4 GiB combined peak RSS. The method must use the public target to
restrict several still-free pair domains before enumerating their Cartesian
product. This is the next measurable contribution needed for a complete IC
attack: a smaller Boolean circuit, a fast inverse for a proposed pair output,
or a relation assembled from saved leaves does not establish ordinary-query
yield.

The longer objective is a **verified single-target DLP with lower online wall
time than a same-point, same-resource Pollard-rho solve**. Once ordinary
queries yield verified, rank-increasing rows, measure the number and cost of
queries required to solve the factor-base logs, target descent, scalar
recovery, and independent scalar replay. Give the complete method a canonical
curve/candidate/workload/run identity under [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).
The headline interval starts with target-dependent work after reusable setup
and ends with a recovered, independently verified scalar. Keep failed
queries, setup, final relation-matrix work, memory, and verification timing
as separate exclusive phase records. CPU speedup claims require the
[host-isolation receipt](../../docs/ISOLATED_BENCHMARKS.md).

## Selected geometry and evidence

The first exact target-search policy is the [same-rotation d13 six-pair
base](n131_d13_same_rotation_pairs_20261009/RESULT.md) on
`EC1N131Ckb1heb5f1998f20b`: 4,134 valid leaf codes per unrotated position,
`B=1,083,108` actual subgroup-usable base points, and `K=4,134` folded
columns. Each of the six pair blocks has at most 34,171,644 signed output
states. The [pair-output pilot](n131_d13_batch_pair_outputs_20261009/RESULT.md)
independently replayed 524,992 outputs; its measured generation cost and
358,802,262-byte raw full-domain payload bound make an exact pair primitive
plausible within memory, while leaving the multi-pair search open. The
[joint inverse](n131_d13_joint_linear_pair_inverse_20261009/RESULT.md)
recovers the seed sum of a proposed output through a 131-by-104 binary
system, with an exact fallback on rank loss. Its per-output cost is a local
stage diagnostic.

The [six-pair frontier](n131_d13_six_pair_prefix_frontier_20261009/RESULT.md)
sets the work test. Under its uniform-target law, fixing two pair outputs
without target guidance leaves at least 249,560,561 complete four-free-pair
probes necessary for 50% success; a 600-second run would need more than
415,934 *complete* probes per second. The [full affine-span
certificate](n131_d13_pair_output_linear_span_20261009/RESULT.md) also
excludes a universal affine filter on one pair-output coordinate. Therefore
the next candidate must couple output or seed-sum constraints across pair
blocks to the target. A different exact base or decomposition is welcome if
it supplies a stronger measured end-to-end path and its own frozen geometry.
The frontier's preserved protocol prints a different curve ID from its
hashed d13 candidate manifest; reconcile that provenance label before
promoting it into a canonical candidate comparison. Its integer support
bound uses the frozen subgroup order and selector count stated above.

## Next experiment and promotion decision

1. Build an exact joint propagator for at least four still-free pair blocks.
   Use the public point in the propagation equations; retain the selected
   pair domains implicitly. Count target-dependent operations and peak
   storage, including construction, failed branches, and exact checks.
   Record how many partial families are removed while at least two pair
   domains still have unchosen leaves. Independent checked-Sage arithmetic
   must replay positive controls and any output relation.
2. Freeze its source, point, input law, seed, resource limits, and baseline.
   On the [N41 same-rotation public control](NEXT_TARGET_COUPLED_BLOCK_GOAL.md),
   recover an unpinned eight-point row within 120 seconds and 4 GiB. Verify
   all leaf membership, eight distinct folded columns, subgroup sum, and
   saved-formula consistency. Preserve every timeout and zero-yield attempt.
3. Give a source-bound N131 operation and memory projection for the **whole
   search**, including target-conditioned generation and exact completion.
   Reject a method whose measured inner-loop rate cannot meet its required
   coverage under the declared input law. Only then freeze one previously
   unseen degree-131 public point and run the 600-second/4-GiB ordinary
   relation gate, followed by independent relation and subgroup replay.
4. If the N131 row gate passes, collect a frozen ordinary-query panel and
   measure verified relation yield, novel rank per query, and cost per useful
   row. Advance to factor logs and one-target scalar recovery only when
   those measurements support the complete pipeline. Pair the final online
   interval with one rho solve on the same public point and resource
   envelope.

The existing [N41 second-link lookup
control](n41_phi_second_link_control_20261009/RESULT.md) verifies exact
propagation but both ordinary searches reached their 120-second caps. The
[target-coupled group join](n41_phi_target_coupled_pair_join_20261009/RESULT.md)
found and replayed an ordinary N41 relation, but its uniform-target N131
transfer bound is too small for the direct sampled join. These outcomes
select **joint target-conditioned pair generation** as the next experiment,
with the N41 row and N131 whole-search projection as explicit decision gates.
