# Nonlinear coordinate trees leave the N41 seven-seed frontier at its frequency baseline

A frozen 32-tree, depth-six multiclass forest tested whether nonlinear public-point or known-prefix coordinates identify the generating seed of the first constrained four-atom block. On the same 20,000 held-out N41 relation-source rows used by the earlier bitwise model, its seven-seed decisions hit **4,459–4,466** labels, compared with **4,465** for the training-frequency selector. The strongest measured difference was one row out of 20,000. This particular nonlinear selector supplies no useful target-conditioned concentration for the W18 seed-budget gate.

The source is the exact twelve-column W6 relation law on `EC1N41Ckb1h023d9a38bbf7`, `y²+xy=x³+1` over `GF(2)[t]/(t^41+t³+1)`, with subgroup order `549756390943`, **B=384** usable points, and **K=26** folded columns. The archived generator sampled two free signed pair states and two same-seed four-atom blocks, retained tuples with block-seed XOR in `0,…,7` and twelve distinct columns, and recorded their public sum. The first 80,000 accepted rows train each forest; the final 20,000 are held out. The label is the *generating* block-A seed; a point can admit additional valid seeds. The [frozen protocol](protocol.json) binds the exact row file, extraction, binary, source, model settings, and 300-second/1-GiB/one-worker cap. This is a selector experiment with `candidate_id: null` and `target_count: 0`.

Each tree is oblivious: one feature bit is selected at each depth by multiclass Gini gain over all current leaves. At each level it considers 16 deterministic pseudorandom feature positions. Each final leaf estimates the 63 class probabilities with 64 training-frequency pseudocounts; the 32 tree probabilities are added and the top seven seeds are returned. Four feature sets were frozen before the held-out run: public `(x,y)` bits; `(Q−P₀)` bits after the first free pair; `(Q−P₀−P₁)` bits after both free pairs; and all six coordinates jointly. The [source](forest.cpp) reads only the first 80,000 labels during training.

The exact paired discordances and normal 95% intervals are in the [audit](audit.json). For the one-pair residual the interval on the recall difference is **−0.065 to +0.075 percentage points**. The joint arm changed only 87 of 20,000 seven-seed sets, which limits what this regularized forest can establish about other nonlinear models. The [capacity control](capacity_control.json) encoded the generating seed in six public x bits in both train and holdout rows; the same forest then included the seed in **20,000/20,000** public-coordinate top-seven lists and **19,254/20,000** joint lists. The [leakage control](leakage_control.json) changed every held-out label while keeping training rows and all coordinates fixed; all 20,000 proposals stayed identical.

The native producer completed in **14.402 local seconds** with **8,880,128 B** peak child RSS. This interval includes training and prediction, with the archived data generation and residual construction outside it; it is an unisolated stage diagnostic. The [run receipt](run_receipt.json) records binary/input hashes, status, stdout, stderr, predictions digest, cap, and sampled RSS. The independent [audit source](audit.py) checks all 20,000 row labels against the archived input, every top-seven list, the prior baseline, raw predictions, paired outcomes, source hashes, and resource caps. The positive-control and label-leakage scripts regenerate their temporary inputs and retain only small hash-bound results.

The W18 N131 support frontier requires roughly **35,732-fold** conditional concentration for seven seeds across sixteen fixed prefixes to reach a half-coverage union ceiling. This panel is generated from valid relation tuples, so its recall is a diagnostic of one proposed selector under that law rather than an ordinary-target relation rate. The next method should impose exact, target-coupled S3 and source-membership constraints on *joint* block families before choosing their seed labels; a useful N41 result must free the witness masks and independently replay a public-point relation before N131 promotion.
