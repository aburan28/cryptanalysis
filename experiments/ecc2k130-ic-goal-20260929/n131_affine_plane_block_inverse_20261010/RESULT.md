# Affine-plane four-atom inverse for ECC2K index calculus

An affine-plane restriction turns the same-seed four-atom output test into one polynomial GCD per fixed seed, offset, and output. Write the two source reciprocal-coordinate pairs as `(w, w+s)` and `(w+c, w+s+c)`. Their products obey

`p=w^2+s*w`, `q=(w+c)^2+s*(w+c)=p+c^2+s*c`.

Substituting this identity in the archived universal four-atom resultant gives a nonzero 518-term polynomial `H(s,c,p,z)` with degree 16 in `p` and degree 8 in the output `z`; it is invariant under `p -> p+c^2+s*c`. For the product set of one frozen source subspace and seed, `gcd(H(s,c,p,z), S_s(p))` finds every product candidate represented by the source. Roots still undergo source-membership, distinct-column, and elliptic-curve group replay. This replaces a product-by-product resultant test at **fixed** `s,c,z`; finding useful target-conditioned outputs and seeds remains a separate algorithmic step.
