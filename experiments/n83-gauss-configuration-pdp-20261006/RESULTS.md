# N83 wide-Gaussian solver-policy gate: measured result

The [source-bound ledger](RESULTS.json), regenerated by
[summarize_gauss.py](summarize_gauss.py), checks the frozen input XCNF hashes,
parent receipts, executed source and protocol hashes, checked-Sage runtime
identities, gzip-archived solver output hashes, exact solver/verification wall
sums, sampled CPU and process-tree RSS, and independent Sage replay. Every
new policy and query attempt used the same CryptoMiniSat binary, one
thread, a 120-second internal solver cap, one-million conflict cap, 900-second
external wall cap, and 2 GiB process-tree RSS cap. The earlier default control
had the same solver limits but no sampled process-tree RSS guard. These were
correctness and algorithmic diagnostics on a contended macOS host, with no
host-isolation receipt.

## Fully pinned positive control

All three new policies used the same 9,658,699-byte XCNF with five exact
factor masks and raw factor points supplied. The earlier default policy had
returned `BOUNDED_UNKNOWN`. The two 15,000-column policies also returned
`BOUNDED_UNKNOWN`. Their logs show the roughly 50,000-column XOR matrix was
rejected by the configured column limit. Raising that limit to 60,000 let
CryptoMiniSat activate the `2075 x 50115` matrix and find a SAT model.

| Policy | Internal Gaussian column cap | Solver wall, exploratory | Solver-reported conflicts | Sampled peak RSS | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Default prior control | 1,000 | 216.108 s | — | Unmeasured | Bounded unknown |
| Enlarged window | 15,000 | 124.977 s | 862,043 | 162.5 MiB | Bounded unknown |
| Enlarged, persistent | 15,000 | 123.898 s | 860,508 | 147.8 MiB | Bounded unknown |
| Wide window | 60,000 | 68.538 s | 244,336 | 176.7 MiB | **SAT, full XCNF/group/Sage PASS** |

The wide runner rebuilt the XCNF byte-for-byte, checked every clause and XOR
row in the solver model, verified all five points in the reference binary-curve
group, and independently replayed the factors, measured projected-base
membership, regular additions, raw fiber, and subgroup target in the checked
Sage build. The fully pinned private input is a positive control. Its wall
time is not a natural-query PDP cost.

## Mask-only and public-only queries

The [query protocol](protocol_queries.json) then applied the same wide policy
to the original hash-pinned slope-witness XCNFs. The planted mask-only query
supplied five factor masks but not their lifts. The unpinned planted and all
four ordinary queries were public-only. For those public-only cases, the
runner did not read the private fixture. Each result is retained even when no
model was returned.

| Frozen query | Solver wall, exploratory | Solver-reported conflicts | Sampled peak RSS | Verified relation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Planted, masks only | 164.926 s | 103,105 | 263.2 MiB | None; bounded unknown |
| Planted, public only | 175.487 s | 726,620 | 215.3 MiB | None; bounded unknown |
| Ordinary raw fiber 0 | 139.423 s | 715,868 | 213.2 MiB | None; bounded unknown |
| Ordinary raw fiber 1 | 167.117 s | 720,989 | 231.0 MiB | None; bounded unknown |
| Ordinary raw fiber 2 | 129.084 s | 165,890 | 291.4 MiB | None; bounded unknown |
| Ordinary raw fiber 3 | 159.829 s | 192,770 | 345.0 MiB | None; bounded unknown |

All four ordinary raw fibers are correlated preimages of **one** public
subgroup target. Their solver wall sum was 595.453 seconds, a stage-only,
exploratory total. The bounded completion count is zero; this panel cannot
estimate an independent-query natural relation rate or a finite cost per
useful row. The pairwise wall differences cannot be promoted to CPU speed
ratios without an isolated-host receipt.

The full W3/W4 factor base has B = 1,936,390 distinct usable projected points
and 11,665 signed/Frobenius-folded matrix columns. Its tuple-count diagnostic
is large, but it is neither measured coverage nor solver success. The wide
Gaussian result isolates a concrete solver issue in the fully pinned circuit.
The unresolved task is choosing unknown factors and lifts on ordinary queries.
The earlier N37 factorized-convolution Hamming-weight pilot likewise found no
model from changing only the weight predicate. The [paper that motivated it](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18866v2)
analyzes MultiSolve for systems with **at most one solution**; a transfer to
this many-decomposition IC setting needs an explicit isolation method and
separate cost and correctness gates.

This pilot does not specify a complete relation collector, factor-log solve,
final relation matrix, target descent, or independently verified one-target
DLP. Its `candidate_id`, IC online wall time, same-point rho online time,
online speedup, cold total, calibrated operation count, and cost per useful
relation remain `null` in the ledger. The fully pinned SAT result is not an
N83 index-calculus speedup.
