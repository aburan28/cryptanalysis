# End-to-end 256-bit carried-gauge τ scalar prototype

## Mathematical path

For secp256k1, choose the cube-root endomorphism `ω(x,y)=(βx,y)`
that agrees with the subgroup eigenvalue `λω`. Set `τ=1-ω` and
`λτ=1-λω (mod n)`. Gauss-reduce the integer kernel generated by
`(n,0)` and `(-λτ,1)`; enumerate the 5×5 neighborhood around the
nearest basis coefficients to obtain a short pair `(a,b)` satisfying
`a+bλτ ≡ k (mod n)`.

Recode `a+bτ` in radix τ using digits from the six units
`±1, ±ω, ±ω²` and zero. Since `τ²=3τ-3`, division after choosing a
digit `(d_a,d_b)` with `a-d_a` divisible by three is

`(a,b) ← (b-d_b+a-d_a, -(a-d_a)/3)`.

Choose the admissible unit minimizing the Eisenstein norm of the
remainder `a²+3ab+3b²`; verify each step strictly decreases the norm
and reconstruct the input pair exactly. A nonzero digit has unit
norm one. For norm at least three, division by τ reduces the norm;
norm-one inputs are themselves admissible unit digits. This gives a
terminating recoding for the tested ring representation.

Evaluate the digits from high to low with explicit Jacobian mixed
addition, τ, and paired-τ formulas. An empty higher digit allows two
τ steps to use the tripling-based paired formula. At each fused pair,
choose the carried unit gauge by minimizing the local Z multiplication
plus digit rotation and final correction, as in the small-field C
candidate. Rotate the next digit into the carried gauge and remove the
final gauge from the output. Sage's built-in `kP` is used only as the
independent expected result.

## Frozen correctness run

Freeze `validate_scalar.py` and this protocol before execution. Its
SHA-256 label fixes four subgroup bases and eight scalars per base:
`0`, `1`, `2`, `n-1`, and four independently derived scalars. Check
the lattice congruence and reconstructed digits, then compare all 32
full scalar outputs with Sage. Also exhaustively test recoding over
the 41×41 small coefficient grid. Preserve each input scalar, short
pair, recode length/weight, output point, and stage counters in
`result.json`. Save the checked launcher runtime before the run:


This is a full correctness prototype, not a competitive or constant
time implementation. The unit digit recoding and tripling identities
have prior art in the supplied Xu–Yu–Han–Lu paper; novelty of the
combined carried-gauge scheme is not established. The script reports
no CPU speedup. A native 256-bit implementation, side-channel review,
and isolated full-operation timing remain separate gates.
