# Point-table capacity for six-unit positional multiplication

Under a 90 MiB cap for 72-byte affine points, **thirteen independent
positional table slots cannot represent every secp256k1 group element** if
each slot selects at most one stored point, applies one of the six
Eisenstein unit maps, and may choose the identity. Fourteen slots pass this
counting requirement, and the verified U14 construction supplies complete
coverage with 1,004,904 stored point slots and 13 mixed additions for a
nonzero digit in every window. The bound identifies the table and operation
model in which U14 has the minimum possible slot count.

Let slot `i` have `n_i` separately charged stored points. It offers at most
`1 + 6 n_i` choices: an implicit identity and six unit images per point.
Unit actions on an intermediate accumulator distribute over the sum, so
they do not enlarge this bound. With `T` fixed slots and a total of `B`
stored points, the number of reachable sums is at most

`product_i (1 + 6 n_i) <= (1 + 6 B/T)^T`.

For integer `n_i`, the product is largest when the table sizes differ by
at most one. Indeed, transferring a point from a table of size at least two
larger to the smaller table strictly increases the product. The
[integer certificate](unit-orbit-capacity-bound.json), generated by
[the checker](unit_orbit_capacity_bound.py), uses the exact secp256k1
subgroup order and checks the balanced product without floating-point
arithmetic. At 90 MiB, `B <= 1,310,720`. For `T=13`, even the maximum
product is below the group order. Reaching the group order with thirteen
slots requires at least **1,835,553 stored points**, or **132,159,816
point bytes (126.037 MiB)**, before maps, metadata, and allocator costs.
Any construction with fewer slots can be padded with identity-only slots,
so the thirteen-slot failure covers every smaller fixed slot count.

The proof applies to fixed positional slots whose table entries are
charged separately. It does not cover shared additive bases, adaptive
selection from a larger table family, compressed points, transformations
other than the six units, or online point operations besides adding the
selected points. Those are the resource models to investigate for a
further reduction in addition count or memory. The existing
[U14 construction](UNIT_ORBIT_WINDOWS_RESULT.md) proves coverage by exact
integer recoding and validates every stored point independently; the
counting argument alone would not prove a construction exists.
