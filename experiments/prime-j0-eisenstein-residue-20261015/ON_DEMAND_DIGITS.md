# Scalar-local Eisenstein digit synthesis

The `31+32τ` sparse radix can evaluate one secp256k1 scalar without preparing its 1,169-seed point table. The [hash-guarded native overlay](native_sparse_on_demand_overlay.py) first performs the exact signed-pair recoding, collects the distinct digit pairs used by that scalar, materializes only their points at `G`, and evaluates the same twenty-step Horner chain. The selected point set includes the terminal pair and a zero slot. The frozen first target and the 128-point pilot each needed at most **22 selected entries**. This is an algorithmic reduction in point-table preparation for a cold operation; it does not establish a peak-memory bound because temporary multiple arrays and maps are also allocated.

## Same-point cold panel

The [committed manifest](tier1-tau32-demand-cold-cpu40-manifest.json) and [predeclared rules](tier1-tau32-demand-cold-cpu40-rules.json) pair the on-demand path with the full-table `k=5` Horner reference on the same frozen scalar and CPU 40 of RunPod pod `thyvmdqmzsz5ik`. Sixteen alternating repetitions each have reference/reference A/A controls immediately before and after the comparison. The cold interval is the Rust `Instant` sum of table or selector preparation and scalar evaluation. Fixture loading, process launch, and output verification are outside that interval for both paths. The candidate places its scalar-local synthesis inside `online_ms` and reports zero separate preparation; the reference reports the full table in `preparation_ms`. The worker's direct `online_ms` ratio is therefore not the cold comparison.

The serial job `a477a7b5198c4a9fa4db825ae0ce03ca` completed **96 valid arms and 48 valid pairs**, with stable source/binary hashes and every A/A ratio inside the predeclared `[0.90,1.11]` interval. The [independent auditor](analyze_tier1_tau32_demand_cold.py) checked raw stdout/stderr, every paired command and point, source and executable hashes, the complete fixture, and an independent scalar multiplication for the target. The full-table reference averaged **54.02 ms** cold and the on-demand candidate averaged **3.24 ms** cold. The qualified paired reference/candidate median was **16.7364×**, with predeclared paired bootstrap 95% interval **[16.6712,16.8610]**. This is a Tier 1 cloud-instance result for one cold scalar on the observed allocation.

The [launch receipt](tier1-tau32-demand-cold-cpu40-launch.json), [audit](tier1-tau32-demand-cold-cpu40-audit.json), [raw archive](tau32-demand-cold-tier1-raw.tar.gz), and [native provenance](tau32-demand-native-provenance.tar.gz) retain the exact evidence. The raw and provenance SHA-256 digests are `06be2253fdb315a3a1bb5654802d4726120317ac17380b9e490f50a045fae16b` and `b057cf543a98a0479bce1de1cc46a4f7710990849488c3cccc3675239af0fc6a`.

## Frozen full-fixture replay

The [full-fixture manifest](tier1-tau32-demand-full-replay-cpu40-manifest.json) and [rules](tier1-tau32-demand-full-replay-cpu40-rules.json) sent two identical candidate arms through the serial worker. The native binary checked each of the **12,288** outputs against the frozen fixture. Job `22a05f971a814d459e3ec804fe967539` completed both arms with stable artifacts and exact output checks. The [independent auditor](analyze_tier1_tau32_demand_full_replay.py), [launch](tier1-tau32-demand-full-replay-cpu40-launch.json), [audit](tier1-tau32-demand-full-replay-cpu40-audit.json), and [raw archive](tau32-demand-full-replay-raw.tar.gz) retain this correctness evidence. The two candidate intervals were **33.875 s** and **33.903 s**. This A/A replay answers correctness and cost shape; it is not a matched throughput comparison against the full-table reference.

The next implementation step is to synthesize the selected digit points directly with the native `U256Jacobian` field path and a shared dyadic basis for `G` and `τG`. The current scalar-local builder invokes the older pair-field window constructor and performs its affine conversions for every scalar. A direct projective builder can remove that per-scalar conversion work while retaining the same recoding and one-scalar cold boundary. Any speed ranking for that change requires a new frozen same-point Tier 1 panel.
