# Paired N83 factor-encoding stage result

The [machine ledger](COST_COMPARISON.json) is regenerated by
[compare_costs.py](compare_costs.py) from the immutable direct-mask and
indexed-factor receipts. It verifies the same curve, public fixture,
measured W3/W4 factor base, solver binary, one-thread limit, conflict cap,
120-second internal solver limit, 900-second outer cap, and 2 GiB process-tree
RSS cap. Every direct run's exclusive build, XCNF write, solver, inner
residual, and outer residual times sum exactly to its process-tree wall time
in integer nanoseconds. The runs used a contended host and have no isolation
receipt; CPU wall ratios are exploratory only.

Both methods used the same **B = 1,936,390** distinct usable projected base
points and **11,665** signed/Frobenius-folded columns. The indexed method
represents each factor by four ordered seven-bit position codes, with a
sentinel for weight three, while the direct-mask method uses 83 mask bits and
an exact cardinality circuit. The point arithmetic is otherwise the same
regular-locus deterministic circuit.

The four ordinary fibers are correlated preimages of **one** target. They
cannot supply a four-query binomial yield estimate. The tested completion
fraction is zero under the frozen cap, while mathematical decomposition
coverage and natural relation yield remain unknown. The indexed factor
encoding reduced primary mask-choice bits and 4,160 total XCNF variables,
but increased CNF clauses by 57,025 on the pinned branch. It did not improve
the bounded unpinned outcome. The differing whole-run wall times cannot
establish a CPU speed difference on this unisolated host.

Neither prototype has a complete relation collector, matrix, verified factor
logs, target descent, scalar recovery, or same-point rho run. Their
`candidate_id`, `IC_online`, rho online time, cost per useful relation, cold
total, and speedup remain `null` in the ledger. The next useful circuit gate
is a material arithmetic change: enforce affine point lifts and regular
additions with bilinear multiplication equations and explicit slope
witnesses, avoiding the five factor-x and four addition-denominator
inversion chains that dominate both current circuits. It must pass planted,
small-field equivalence, unpinned, and ordinary controls before any IC
promotion.
