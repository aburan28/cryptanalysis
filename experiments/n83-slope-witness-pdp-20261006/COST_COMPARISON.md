Relative to the indexed circuit on the same pinned fiber, the slope circuit
has **473,598 fewer variables (78.0%)**, **461,563 fewer AND gates (78.8%)**,
**1,384,357 fewer CNF clauses (75.9%)**, and **36,387,482 fewer XCNF bytes
(79.0%)**. These are exact circuit counts, not solve-time speedups. The slope
pinned run's sampled process-tree peak was 281.0 MiB versus 633.3 MiB for
indexed; memory and wall were observed on a contended, unisolated host.

The slope ordinary fibers consumed 163.570, 141.672, 124.787, and 125.030
seconds of whole process-tree wall time. The same four fibers are correlated
preimages of **one** public subgroup target; they cannot provide a four-query
binomial yield estimate. The observed bounded completion fraction is zero
under the frozen cap. Mathematical decomposition coverage, natural relation
yield, novel rank per query, and cost per useful row remain unknown.

Every completed attempt's exclusive circuit-build, XCNF-write, solver,
inner-residual, and outer-residual durations are nonnegative integers whose
sum exactly equals its process-tree wall duration. The raw bounded solver logs
are gzip archived and verified against the stdout hashes in their receipts;
XCNF source, generated XCNF hash, runtime identity, memory peak, and failed
attempt status are retained. The 216.530-second fully pinned solver control
also returned `BOUNDED_UNKNOWN`. A separate deterministic evaluation of the
known planted witness assigned all 133,198 Boolean variables and verified all
438,558 CNF clauses and 6,644 XOR rows; independent checked Sage replay
passed. It proves that the circuit admits that witness, while the solver
outcome shows this encoding did not find it at the matched cap.

None of these pilots has a complete relation collector, factor-base log
system, final relation matrix solve, target descent, verified one-target
scalar recovery, or same-point rho solve. Thus `candidate_id`, IC online wall
time, rho online wall time, `rho_online / IC_online`, cold total, calibrated
operation count, and controlled CPU speedup are `null` in the ledger. A
stage wall sum cannot replace any of them. A future attempt must preserve
all failed target-dependent searches in the one-target bill and obtain an
isolated-host receipt before promoting a CPU speedup claim.
