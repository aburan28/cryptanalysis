# ECC2K-130 Frobenius GPU stalls have no confirmed production gain

- **Status:** `investigating`
- **Kind:** empirical bottleneck; missing validated structural improvement
- **Affected work:** [initial GPU optimization report](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261013/README.md), [source-correlated follow-up](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/README.md), [production runner](../ecc2k130/runner/README.md)
- **Next action owner:** ECC2K-130 GPU engineering
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-14

## Problem

The current measurements do not identify a source change that improves production-compatible ECC2K-130 Frobenius-walk throughput. A source-correlated profile now localizes sampled synchronization stalls to grouped inversion and short dependency stalls near the carryless product fold. Three subsequent candidates did not pass their same-allocation A/A advancement gate. The missing step is a correctness-checked structural change that reduces the actual critical dependency or synchronization cost without increasing another bottleneck.

The affected workload is the `certicom-ecc2k130-frobenius-v1` walk at pinned source `1f8e6847de9e207880bd3be63835c3f56eb182d5`: curve 131, batch 16, 120,320 workers, 640-thread blocks, four-warp grouped inversion, run ID 11000 and DP32. A production improvement must preserve the sorted DP corpus, zero-drop behavior, arithmetic, and fresh/checkpoint/resume state, then beat a same-allocation paired baseline beyond A/A noise. A local instruction-count or profiler change alone does not meet that requirement.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Observed:** The [isolated 64-step profile](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261013/production-compute-profile-isolated.json) and [raw-counter profile](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261013/production-stall-profile.json) used CUDA 13.3, driver 610.57.04 and one RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition. They reported 89.57% SM compute throughput, 41.11% achieved occupancy, 96 registers/thread, zero local spilling requests and 16 barrier slots. Raw stalled-warps-per-active-issue-cycle ratios were 1.645498 for barriers and 1.618835 for math-pipe throttle. All 22 application-replay passes had fresh DP files and matching output. `SourceCounters` did not provide a usable printed per-instruction stall breakdown.
- **Observed:** Frozen paired screens against the pinned source retained the 355-record sorted DP32 SHA-256 `c80bb8db34e419d53bc946a5fef01c99fd60214a16f9b42ff87b5d52f3668a90` with zero drops. [Full top CLMAD](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261013/top-clmad-screen.json) had median paired ratio 0.839956; [half-top CLMAD](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261013/top-half-clmad-screen.json) 0.930688; [zip-based normal square](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261013/zip-square-screen.json) 0.997502; [static 640-thread barriers](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261013/static-barrier-screen.json) 0.992947 and static 128-thread geometry 0.856411. [Root-only barrier removal](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261013/root-barrier-screen.json) was inside its A/A spread. These are two-pair screens, not five-pair confirmation results. Host field and walk checks passed for the new half-top and zip-square arithmetic switches.
- **Observed:** The [source-correlated Nsight receipt](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/source-correlated-profile.json) retained the [24,026,820-byte native report](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/source-correlated-profile.ncu-rep), executable and report hashes, imported SASS and CUDA/SASS tables, and 22 ordered replay passes with 22 distinct DP files and zero drops. The [independently recomputed hotspot table](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/source-hotspots.json) counted 294,473 not-issued warp samples: math throttle 106,100, wait 79,014, barrier 62,685 and short scoreboard 25,410. Two PCs in `collective_inverse.cuh` hold 29,624 and 26,563 barrier samples. Several `LOP3.LUT` consumers around the `clmad.hi` source line have 2,000–2,761 short-scoreboard samples each. A sampled carryless-product PC prints as `???` in this profiler version and maps to two source lines, so its precise instruction identity remains ambiguous. These are sampled stalls, not wall-time shares; the attribution points to consumers that may be waiting on earlier producers.
- **Observed:** Three new paired production screens kept the frozen DP32 corpus with zero drops and passed independent [rate/corpus validation](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/validate_screen.py). [High-half-first CLMAD](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/clmad-hi-first-screen.json) had candidate ratios 1.001777 and 1.001109 against A/A 1.001268 and 0.998798; one candidate pair remained inside the A/A interval. [Same-group 256-thread geometry](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/w4-256-screen.json) had ratios 0.597903 and 0.598083 against A/A 1.000246 and 1.000839. [Three-product high-half batching](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/clmul-hi-batch-screen.json) had ratios 0.995671 and 1.000616 against A/A 1.000254 and 1.002569. Both source changes passed the host arithmetic and walk reference gate. No candidate passed its two-pair advancement rule; none reached five-pair confirmation.
- **Derived or proved:** The listed variants did not satisfy their predeclared advancement gate under this exact workload. No hardware lower bound or universal limit follows from the screens.
- **Suspected explanation:** Math-pipe pressure, inverse barriers and dependency latency interact. A local reorder may be absorbed by ptxas or trade shorter scoreboard waits for longer live ranges. The `FP64` pipeline label alone does not map carryless SASS instructions to a physical execution unit.
- **Missing capability:** A proved and GPU-validated way to reduce grouped-inverse synchronization or the repeated carryless product dependency without changing walk outputs, plus a same-allocation paired result beyond A/A noise.
- **Access/tool limit:** The [double-buffer candidate gate](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/inverse-double-buffer-gate.json) built with 96 registers/thread and 27,392 bytes of shared memory, and its independent device inverse checks passed at 512 and 640 threads. Compute Sanitizer 2026.2.1.0 then returned `Error: Device not supported` on the RTX PRO 6000 before analyzing the kernel. This is no race finding and leaves the frozen race gate open. Its [raw log](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/inverse-double-buffer-gate-r2.log) and the earlier local import failure are retained.
- **Checker controls:** A [race-free 64-thread Blackwell control](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/sanitizer-blackwell-control.json) also returned `Device not supported`. On an H100, the [same checker](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/sanitizer-h100-control.json) reported zero hazards for a clean control, detected a planted write-after-write hazard, and reported zero hazards for the unchanged 640-thread double-buffer inverse test. These controls narrow the tool limit to the allocated Blackwell environment and provide cross-device evidence, but do not close the Blackwell race gate. The attempted confidential-compute-mode query failed, leaving that cause unknown.
- **Exploratory timing:** The [double-buffer screen](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/inverse-double-buffer-exploratory-screen.json) reproduced the 355-record DP32 corpus with zero drops in every arm. [Independent validation](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/inverse-double-buffer-exploratory-validation.json) computed A/A ratios `0.999955`, `1.001893` and candidate ratios `0.999034`, `1.007698`. One candidate pair fell below the A/A interval, so the advancement gate failed. The change stays off and the Blackwell checker gap remains.
- **What this evidence does not establish:** It does not rule out other source changes, another launch geometry, a different compiler or GPU, or a useful speedup. It does not establish an end-to-end cloud or ECDLP attack-cost advantage. The direct KernelAgent integration gap is tracked separately in [the CUDA bridge problem](ecc2k130-kernelagent-cuda-bridge.md).

## What would unblock it

Each replay pass used a fresh DP file and preserved the expected profile output with zero drops. The source table names grouped-inverse barrier PCs and carryless-product consumers; one carryless PC still prints as `???` and maps to two source lines.
- [ ] Implement a default-off source change targeted at that sequence; pass independent host arithmetic, GPU DP32 corpus and fresh/checkpoint/resume checks, then exceed the A/A range in both 64-launch screening pairs and improve all five warmed 128-launch confirmation pairs against the pinned production build.

The first condition makes the next optimization choice reviewable; it is not a speed result. The second would establish a same-GPU useful-update improvement for the frozen walk. Cloud cost, other GPUs, long-run DP collection, and any ECDLP attack-cost claim would still need separate accounting.

## Next discriminating step

- **Action or proof obligation:** Screen two-warp inverse groups at the same 640-thread block geometry as the pinned four-warp production walk. The group tree has one level instead of two and retains the original final barrier, but uses ten root inversions per block instead of five. This isolates synchronization depth from the earlier 320-thread two-warp geometry change. Compare compiled resources, run the independent 640-thread two-warp inverse reference, then use the frozen DP32 corpus and same-allocation A/A timing screen. Keep the double-buffer result as a separate, bounded observation.
- **Current gate:** The source and synchronization argument are in the [candidate report](../ecc2k130/runner/research/kernelagent-20261014/README.md), independent device arithmetic passed, and the H100 checker passed calibrated controls and the candidate. The Blackwell checker remains unavailable, and the separately frozen exploratory Blackwell screen did not pass its A/A advancement rule. A supported Blackwell checker or separately reviewed equivalent validation gate would clear the missing correctness obligation for this candidate, but a different structural candidate is now needed for the measured throughput goal.
- **Inputs and controls:** Same pinned production walk, CUDA 13.3 `sm_120`, run ID 11000, 120,320 workers, 640 threads/block, batch 16 and DP32. Change only `COLLECTIVE_WARPS=2` in the candidate; keep the four-warp baseline and production A/A arm on one Modal RTX PRO 6000 allocation. Require the exact 355-record sorted corpus and zero drops in every timing arm. Record source/binary hashes, resource counts, commands, raw logs and a human-readable insight report.
- **Execution limits and stop condition:** Bound each compilation/check to one GPU allocation. Stop before timing on an inverse mismatch, changed corpus, nonzero drops or resource admission failure; preserve the actual outcome. A 64-launch candidate advances only if both paired ratios exceed its A/A interval; then run five warmed 128-launch pairs and fresh/checkpoint/resume compatibility checks.
- **Outcomes and next actions:** A corpus-preserving result beyond A/A supports confirmation only for this launch geometry. A correct but slower result shows that the extra root inversions outweigh the reduced tree depth under this workload; then return to arithmetic or scheduling changes. An invalid or ambiguous result leaves the comparison open. A profiler or Modal failure is infrastructure evidence only.
- **Recheck trigger:** The same-640 two-warp screen, a supported Blackwell race checker for the separate double-buffer candidate, a compiler change that exposes the ambiguous carryless PC, or another arithmetic reduction with a frozen paired production screen.
